# Harvest Notes

## Blossom
The orchard rows run north to south so the blossom catches the morning sun evenly. Frost during blossom week can halve the yield, so the smudge pots are stacked and ready from the first week of April until the petals fall.

## Picking
Picking crews start at dawn and fill bins by variety, never mixing. During the harvest festival the village hangs a paper lantern from every tenth tree along the lane, and picking pauses for the evening market. Ladders are inspected weekly and any cracked rung is burned, not repaired.

## Storage
Fruit stores best at two degrees with the humidity held high. The cold room vents are opened on still nights to draw in cool air. Any bin showing rot is removed whole; a single bad apple in storage is treated as a fault of the whole bin.
