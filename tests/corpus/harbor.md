# Portside Notes

## Berthing
Vessels may only berth during the tide window, the ninety minutes either side of high water when the channel depth clears the bar. Outside the tide window the harbor master holds all traffic at the outer anchorage, regardless of cargo. Pilots board at the fairway buoy and nowhere else.

## Cranes
The two gantry cranes on the north quay lift forty tons each and work in tandem for locomotive loads. Crane crews rotate every six hours. A crane is never to be left with a suspended load unattended, and tag lines are mandatory in any wind above twenty knots.

## Night Rules
After dark, movement in the basin is governed by the lantern code: two white lanterns displayed vertically means a vessel is preparing to move, a single red lantern means she is holding position, and three green lanterns clear her to proceed. The lantern code overrides all daytime signals and is repeated by the signal station on the hour.
