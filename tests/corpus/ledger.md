# Stores Ledger

## Rope
The rope store holds manila and hemp in coils of twenty fathoms. Every coil issued is entered in the ledger against the vessel that drew it, and frayed rope returned from sea is condemned without exception and sold as oakum.

## Oil
Lamp oil is drawn by the pint and signed for. The storekeeper keeps two grades: a thin oil for the signal lamps and a heavy oil for machinery. Lantern oil shortages last winter forced the port to ration the thin grade to one pint per lamp per week.

## Canvas
Sail canvas is issued by the bolt and cut only on the long table. Offcuts longer than a yard are returned to the bolt store and recorded; shorter offcuts go to the patching bin for the sailmakers' apprentices to practice on.
